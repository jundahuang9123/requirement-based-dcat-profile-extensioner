\paragraph{Measures.} We report two views of every run. From the reference: Recovery and Strict over the aligned
reference elements~$N$ (Sect.~\ref{sec:benchmark}), and the agreement of matched pairs on each facet. From the
output: the included changes, by kind, and the share of them that match a reference element. This share says how
much of an extension one published modeling accounts for; it is a precision against that modeling, not the share
of valid changes, and the changes it leaves unmatched are adjudicated from pooled outputs instead of being counted
as errors \cite{buckley2004incomplete,zobel1998reliable}. Each criterion adds its own counts. For E1: the extension
rate, minted properties over included property-level changes (class changes and usage notes left out, as the code
counts), beside the reference delta's own rate, and the unjustified mints, generated mints where the reference
reuses or refines. % src: @5ffd56e B/engine/pipeline.py:463, 473; NOTES 4; PLAN:70
For E2: the trace failures and the judge's verdicts. For E3: the changes auto-accepted, the decisions by who made
them, and the \emph{review load}, the changes in the queue and the open conflicts at the end of a run, a count, not
a measure of effort; atoms left unclear or unaddressed are counted apart, because they never enter the queue.
% src: ENG §5, §7.11; @5ffd56e B/engine/pipeline.py:430-449
For E4: the conformance verdicts, the failing and warning rules, the witness warnings of C7 by cause, and the terms
of a later \ac{DCAT} sent to a person. We also give atom coverage by the tool's own count, in which an atom is
addressed when an included change claims to cover it in full, the model's own claim; stability, read from the
ranges, from the reference elements credited in all three runs against those credited in at least one, and from
the mean pairwise Jaccard overlap of the credited sets; and cost, as model calls without cached replays, input
tokens and hours. A sum over cases adds each case's median run, so larger cases count for more. \todo{behavior on
