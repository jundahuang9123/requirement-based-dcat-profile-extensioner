\paragraph{Measures.} We report two views of every run. From the reference: Recovery and Strict over~$N$
(Sect.~\ref{sec:benchmark}) and the facet agreement of matched pairs. From the output: the included changes by kind
and the share that match a reference element, a precision against one published modeling, not the share of valid
changes; unmatched changes are adjudicated from pooled outputs \cite{buckley2004incomplete,zobel1998reliable}. Per
criterion: E1, the extension rate (minted properties over included property-level changes) beside the reference
delta's, unjustified mints (where the reference reuses or refines) and Recovery by element kind; % src: @5ffd56e B/engine/pipeline.py:463, 473; NOTES 4; PLAN:70
E2, trace failures and the judge's verdicts; E3, auto-accepted changes, decisions by who made them and the
\emph{review load}, the changes queued and conflicts open at the end of a run, a count, not a measure of effort;
unclear or unaddressed atoms, which never enter the queue, are counted apart; % src: ENG §5, §7.11; @5ffd56e B/engine/pipeline.py:430-449
E4, conformance verdicts, failing and warning rules, C7 warnings by cause and terms of a later \acs{DCAT} sent to a
person. Also reported are atom coverage by the tool's own count (\emph{addressed} is the model's own claim of full
coverage), stability (ranges, elements credited in every run and in at least one, mean pairwise Jaccard
overlap) and cost (calls without cached replays, input tokens, hours). Sums over cases take each case's median run. \todo{behavior on test
