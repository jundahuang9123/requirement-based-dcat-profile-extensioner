\paragraph{Measures.} We report two views of every run. From the reference: Recovery and Strict over~$N$
(Sect.~\ref{sec:benchmark}) and the facet agreement of matched pairs. From the output: the included changes by kind
and the share that match a reference element, a precision against one published modeling, not the share of valid
changes; unmatched changes are adjudicated from pooled outputs \cite{buckley2004incomplete,zobel1998reliable}. Per
criterion: for E1, the extension rate, minted properties over included property-level changes as the code counts
it, beside the reference delta's rate, and the unjustified mints, where the reference reuses or refines;
% src: @5ffd56e B/engine/pipeline.py:463, 473; NOTES 4; PLAN:70
for E2, trace failures and the judge's verdicts; for E3, the changes auto-accepted, the decisions by who made them
and the \emph{review load}, the changes in the queue and open conflicts at the end of a run, a count, not a measure
of effort, with atoms left unclear or unaddressed counted apart because they never enter the queue;
% src: ENG §5, §7.11; @5ffd56e B/engine/pipeline.py:430-449
for E4, the conformance verdicts, failing and warning rules, C7 warnings by cause and terms of a later \ac{DCAT}
sent to a person. We also report atom coverage by the tool's own count (\emph{addressed} is the model's own claim of
full coverage), stability (the ranges, the elements credited in every run and in at least one, and the mean
pairwise Jaccard overlap) and cost (model calls without cached replays, input tokens, hours). Sums over cases take
each case's median run. \todo{behavior on test records (plan metric): in or out (Q27)}
