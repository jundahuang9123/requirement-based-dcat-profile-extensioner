\section{Discussion and Threats to Validity}\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{The reference delta as one modeling, partly outside the atoms.}
A published profile records one team's modeling against one base release, and several modelings can meet an atom;
relaxed matches and adjudication therefore carry weight, and Strict beside Recovery bounds their effect.
Elements no atom asks for do not count as misses (Sect.~\ref{sec:problem}), and references extracted by hand add % loosened elements kept apart, second person on hand-extracted references: sections/05-benchmark.tex (Reference deltas)
judgment of their own.
Where a published profile mints nothing, every mint departs from it, though it may model its atom validly.

\paragraph{Machine judgments and the final say.}
Dispositions, attribution verdicts and confidence are model judgments, unverified and distinct from a person's
acceptance.
The judge shares the proposer's model, so it is not independent of it, and \ac{LLM} judges are biased and
inconsistent \cite{zheng2023judge,wang2024fair,stureborg2024inconsistent,liu2023geval}.
It is not rerun after consolidation (Sect.~\ref{sec:check}), although it alone weighs facets beyond the obligation % src: @5ffd56e B/engine/pipeline.py:239-243, 279, 359-366 (consolidation keeps the earlier T2 result)
against the atoms. % src: @5ffd56e B/engine/verify.py:442-475 (T1: obligation only)
B1 checks existence and strength, not meaning, and nothing checks out-of-scope decisions (Sect.~\ref{sec:setup}). % src: @5ffd56e B/engine/verify.py:513-533; ENG §3 B1, §7.11
