\section{Discussion and Threats to Validity}\label{sec:discussion}

\paragraph{The reference delta as one modeling, partly outside the atoms.}
A published profile records one team's modeling against one base release, and several modelings can meet an atom,
so relaxed matching and adjudication carry weight; Strict beside Recovery bounds the effect of that judgment.
Reference elements no atom asks for are beyond an atom-driven step and do not count as misses
(Sect.~\ref{sec:problem}); loosened elements are kept apart, and references extracted by hand add judgment.
Where a published profile mints nothing, every mint departs from it, although it may model its atom validly.

\paragraph{From extracted needs to a profile.}
The reference atoms, each reviewed by the companion study's curator, isolate the modeling step \cite{anon2027rq1}.
The extracted atoms come from needs the companion workflow retained and no person accepted, far more than the
reference has atoms; runs on them show how extraction errors reach the profile.

\paragraph{Minting and review cost.}
The extension rate shows how often the pipeline mints.
A term outside the index fails V1, so where a domain's vocabularies are missing, the model must mint or pick another
term; the rules baseline mints below the confidence threshold, so each of its mints waits for a person.

\paragraph{Retrieval and the vocabularies within reach.}

then pushes the model toward a mint; class retrieval, cutting its raw hits before boosting base classes, dropped a
base class in a synthetic probe.

\paragraph{Machine judgments and the final say.}
Dispositions, attribution verdicts and confidence are model judgments: none has been verified, and none is a
person's acceptance.
The judge is the proposer's model, hence not independent of it, and \ac{LLM} judges are biased and
inconsistent \cite{zheng2023judge,wang2024fair,stureborg2024inconsistent,liu2023geval}.
It sees only changes without a symbolic error, blocks only for an unsupported verdict or facet and is not rerun % src: @5ffd56e B/engine/pipeline.py:239-243, 279, 363
after consolidation, although it alone reads facets beyond the obligation against the atoms. % src: @5ffd56e B/engine/verify.py:442-475 (T1: obligation only)
B1 checks that named base elements exist and are strong enough, not what they mean; nothing checks out-of-scope
decisions; the confidence threshold is our setting.

\paragraph{Contamination.}
Training data may have included the published profiles and their documents \cite{sainz2023contamination}.
The closed book shows what the model produces without atoms, which neither proves nor excludes that it saw them;
counterfactual atoms test whether the output follows the atoms or the publication \cite{wu2024reciting}.
